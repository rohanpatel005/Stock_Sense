Introduction to StockSense

StockSense is an AI-powered stock market analysis and paper trading platform designed to help beginners and investors make informed investment decisions.

It combines real-time market data, AI-driven insights, technical analysis, portfolio tracking, and risk-free paper trading into a single platform, making investing simple, intelligent, and accessible.
